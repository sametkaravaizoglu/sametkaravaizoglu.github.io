Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email us at sametkaravaizoglu@gmail.com and include your device model and the level number. We usually answer within two working days.

FAQ

  • A purchase didn't arrive. Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases (No Ads / Starter Pack). For coin packs, write to us with the order number from your App Store / Google Play receipt.
  • How do I remove ads? Buy "No Ads" in the shop. Reward videos stay optional.
  • Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device; uninstalling the app deletes it.
